Mesh & vertical grid

This chapter describes the horizontal mesh and the vertical coordinate, and illustrates the wet–dry behaviour of the shallow upper river.

The mesh is the geometric backbone of the model. hgrid.gr3 (and its lon-lat twin hgrid.ll) define the node coordinates, depths and element connectivity; vgrid.in sets the vertical coordinate. Every other input and output is indexed off this mesh.

Note

The detailed mesh-generation methodology and the per-node *.gr3 spatial fields are still to be documented here — see the planned content below.

Wet and dry in the upper river

A practical consequence of the bathymetry shows up at the head of the estuary: the upper river is shallow and intermittently dry, so the wet channel narrows and widens over the tidal cycle. This matters for the model because the river point source discharges into cells that may be dry or near-dry — the cause of the source-injection issue examined in the conservation testing.
