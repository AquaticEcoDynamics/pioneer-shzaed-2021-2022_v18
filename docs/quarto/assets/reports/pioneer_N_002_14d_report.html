How each term is computed

Term A — Storage (mmol N)

Volume integral of all N-containing state-variable concentrations over the control volume's elements and layers:

M(t) = Σelem∈CV Σlayer c(elem, layer, t) × layer_ht × elem_area × n_per_mol

State pools summed (all in mmol N/m³ after the per-mol conversion): NIT_amm, NIT_nit, OGM_don, OGM_pon, PHY_mixed (×16/106 Redfield N:C).

Term B — Internal rates (mmol N/day)

Volume integral of process rates that are net losses from the total-N pool:

  • NIT_denit (NO3 → N2, sign −1)
  • NIT_anammox (NH4+NO2 → N2, sign −1)

Other rates (nitrification, DNRA, mineralisation, hydrolysis, phyto uptake) are tracked individually but their sign_in_pool = 0 so they cancel in the total-N closure.

Term C — Atmospheric flux (mmol N/day)

Area integral of NIT_din_atm (2D sheet, mmol N/m²/day) over the surface area of the CV.

Term D — Sediment-water flux (mmol N/day)

Sum of 2D sheet diagnostics, area-integrated over the CV:

  • NIT_amm_dsf, NIT_nit_dsf, OGM_don_swi, OGM_pon_swi, PHY_phy_swi_n

All taken as positive into the water column.

Term E — Boundary inflow (mmol N/day)

From flux.out: signed sum of N-tracer transports across the control volume's bounding fluxflag regions:

  • region 3 (sign +1) — Pioneer Mouth (region 3 -> region 2): positive = bay -> estuary, INTO CV

Tracer columns summed (with n-per-mol weighting): NIT_amm, NIT_nit, OGM_don, OGM_pon, PHY_mixed (×16/106).

Term F — Point-source (river) input (mmol N/day)

River nutrient inputs from source_sink.in + vsource.th + msource.th for sources whose target element is interior to the control volume:

F(t) = Σsrc∈CV Σt∈N-pools Qsrc(t) × csrc,t(t) × n_per_mol[t] × 86400

msource.th concentrations are in mmol/m³ (same unit as flux.out), so no extra conversion is needed. Pools summed: NIT_amm, NIT_nit, OGM_don, OGM_pon, PHY_mixed (×16/106).

Closure-check interpretation

If closure residual is small relative to ΔStorage (say < 10%), the budget is closed within numerical accuracy. Common reasons for non-closure:

  • Time-cadence mismatch between scribed and cmb outputs (storage is read on scribed cadence; rates+fluxes on cmb cadence).
  • flux.out tracer ordering mismatched against DEFAULT_TRACER_ORDER in boundary_fluxes.py.
  • fluxflag.prop boundary signs incorrectly specified (positive flux at a transect goes IN one direction; the sign in the region config must reflect whether that direction is into or out of the CV).
  • msource.th tracer-column ordering — Term F assumes the AED state-var registration order in DEFAULT_AED_TRACER_ORDER (point_sources.py); override if your build differs.
